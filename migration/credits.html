The light above Earth · 极光参考

The procedural auroral curtains are original SKC geometry and shaders. Visual research included NASA’s Cruising Past the Aurora Borealis and Purple Haze Aurora. Their photographs and other creators’ shaders are not embedded here. Color palettes, height, magnetic oval and scale are artistic treatments.
